The reward

You may become eligible for one $1,000 reward when a homeowner you referred completes a verified, qualifying solar installation. There is no reward for a form submission, an estimate, a consultation, or a signed contract. There are no other reward amounts.

How a referral counts

  • The referral must come through your personal WattWorth link or code. The code is checked by WattWorth and must belong to a real WattWorth customer.
  • You cannot refer yourself. A referral using your own phone number or email does not count.
  • The first valid referral for a homeowner counts. A homeowner has one referrer.
  • There is one reward per installation.

Verification and payment

WattWorth decides whether an installation is verified and qualifying. The installer reporting an installation is not enough on its own. After WattWorth verifies it, your reward shows as "pending". Share honestly

When you share your link, say that you may receive a reward. Our share message includes this. Do not make savings promises on WattWorth's behalf, and do not send messages to people who have not asked to hear from you.

Privacy

You see only anonymous counts and stages for your referrals. We never show you the homeowner's name, contact details, address, utility information or contract amounts.
