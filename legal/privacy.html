Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

WattWorth, an Arden Home service, collects the information you submit to provide a preliminary solar estimate and respond to your request. This may include your name, contact information, ZIP code, utility provider, electric-bill range, ownership status, and roof details.

How we use information

We use submitted information to generate an estimate, evaluate basic solar fit, maintain lead records, prevent fraud, improve the service, and—when you consent—share the request with the identified independent solar professional for follow-up.

Sharing

We do not publish your information. We may share it with service providers that support hosting and data storage, and with the independent solar professional assigned to respond to your request. We may disclose information when legally required.

Retention and security

We retain lead records only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information by contacting the address listed on the WattWorth website. You may also tell the solar professional not to contact you further.

Contact

Before public launch, replace this paragraph with Arden Home's monitored privacy email and mailing address.
