Built for clarity

WattWorth is a lead and estimate service—not a solar contractor.

We provide preliminary educational estimates and connect interested homeowners with an independent solar professional. Results are not a contract, financing approval, tax opinion, utility guarantee, or final system design.

Questions

Before you begin

Is the estimate exact?

No. It is a preliminary range based on the information you provide and general regional assumptions.

Will WattWorth sell my information?

We use your information to generate your estimate and, when you consent, share it with the named solar professional for follow-up. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. Requesting an estimate or follow-up does not obligate you to purchase solar products or services.
